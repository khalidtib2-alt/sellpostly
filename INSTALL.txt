PRODUCT IDENTITY
extractProductIdentity(title, optionalCategory) cleans common Amazon sales filler and recognizes known Brand + Model patterns, including Sony WH-1000XM5, Apple Watch, Samsung Galaxy, Canon EOS, Nikon, Fujifilm, Garmin, Breville, Nespresso, Logitech and others. Recognized color/storage variants are included in image searches. The original product title is retained in the catalog.
Detection is local and heuristic, not a verified product-database lookup. Matched, inferred and uncertain results are distinguished in code. Ambiguous names and accessory listings are not treated as confirmed exact-model matches. Sellers can correct the identity in Confirm Brand / Model, and that value persists in catalog JSON and shared links.
The ten visual niches are audio, watch, phone-tech, camera, coffee-kitchen, fitness, fashion-bag, beauty-skincare, gaming and home-office.

IMAGE FINDER IN AFFILIATE EDITOR
Below Image URL and the prominent Upload Custom Photo button, the Product Identity & Images panel shows the detected name and provides:
- Official Brand Media Kit: a Google query for the detected model/variant plus official product press-kit images.
- Wikimedia Commons: an image search on Commons for the detected model/variant.
- Free Licensed Photos (Unsplash): a visual-niche lifestyle photo search.
All links open in a new tab; no third-party image search API or automatic scraping/downloading is used.
These are search helpers. Search results do not verify the model or license. The editor asks sellers to confirm exact-model identity, image license, attribution and brand permissions. Unsplash lifestyle results are not presented as guaranteed identical product photographs. Custom seller photos and authorized non-Amazon image URLs remain supported.

TEN STUDIO COVERS
Ten original, local SVG product-showcase scenes use full-bleed gradients, lighting, shadows and niche-specific product illustrations. They contain no brand logos, embedded text, external asset references or scripts. The SVGs total about 21 KB.
Missing Affiliate photos, blocked Amazon CDN images and older category-placeholder images automatically select the closest title/category cover. Cover choice can be overridden with any of the ten one-click thumbnails, or reset with Auto-select Cover. Manual cover selections persist; uploading/pasting a custom photo takes precedence.
Affiliate cards render the covers full-bleed, with a visible Editorial illustration caption. Covers are generic niche artwork, not photographs or precise renders of the named model. Custom product photos remain contained. Personal Seller photos/layout and payment behavior remain separate.
Regenerate the deterministic SVG assets if needed:
node scripts/generate-editorial-covers.mjs
